About Me

I'm Sherif Ashraf — a graphic designer, video editor, and content creator who treats every project as a full production, not just a task.

On the design side, I build brand visuals, 3D commercial graphics, and promotional artwork that give products and businesses a real identity. On the editing side, I handle dynamic cuts, pacing, sound design, and color — turning raw footage into content that actually holds attention.

I don't stop at the technical work either. I've run media buying, marketing, editing, and design for the same project at the same time, which means I understand how a piece of content performs, not just how it looks. And with a background in acting and directing, I bring a director's eye for framing and real, unscripted charisma in front of the camera.

From the first idea to the final export, I like owning the whole process.
